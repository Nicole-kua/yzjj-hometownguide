Flying in from overseas?从国外飞过来？

Batu Pahat has no airport. Fly into Kuala Lumpur, Singapore or Senai (Johor), then pick one of the options below.

峇株吧辖没有机场。可以飞到吉隆坡、新加坡或士乃（柔佛），再从下面的方式选一个过来。

Option 1: fly to KLIA方案一：飞到吉隆坡（KLIA）

About 5 hours on the coach车程约 5 小时

  1. Fly to Kuala Lumpur (KLIA)飞到吉隆坡国际机场（KLIA）Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, both work.第一或第二航站楼都可以。
  2. Take the direct coach to Batu Pahat搭直达巴士到峇株吧辖Airport Coach and Sanwa Express leave from the airport coach bays, with trips from early morning to late night, and stop at Batu Pahat Bus Terminal. Book your seat online before you fly.Airport Coach 和 Sanwa Express 从机场巴士站出发，从清晨到深夜都有班次，终点是峇株巴士总站。建议出发前先上网订票。
  3. Or: private car transfer或者：包车接送Prefer door to door? Book a private car from KLIA straight to your hotel in Batu Pahat, about 3 hours by road. Arrange it before you fly.想要门到门？可以预订包车，从 KLIA 直接载到峇株的酒店，车程约 3 小时。请在出发前安排好。
  4. GrabCar to your hotel叫 GrabCar 到酒店In town, GrabCar gets you between your hotel, the food stops and the banquet hall.在市区内，酒店、美食和迎宾楼之间都可以叫 GrabCar。

Option 2: fly to Singapore or Senai方案二：飞到新加坡或士乃

Then choose: bus from Larkin, or a private car之后二选一：从 Larkin 搭巴士，或包车

  1. Fly to Singapore (Changi) or Senai (JHB)飞到新加坡樟宜机场或士乃机场（JHB）Senai International Airport is in Johor; flights connect from KLIA and other cities.士乃国际机场在柔佛，可从 KLIA 或其他城市转机过来。
  2. Choice A: get to Larkin Sentral bus terminal选择 A：先到 Larkin 巴士总站From Senai, a short GrabCar ride (the airport shuttle goes to JB Sentral, not Larkin). From Singapore, cross the Causeway into Johor Bahru and head to Larkin.从士乃：叫 GrabCar 过去很近（机场巴士只到 JB Sentral，不到 Larkin）。从新加坡：过长堤进入新山，再前往 Larkin。
  3. Then the bus from Larkin to Batu Pahat再从 Larkin 搭巴士到峇株KKKL Express and S&S International run about 20 trips a day, roughly 7:30am to 9pm. You arrive at Batu Pahat Bus Terminal.KKKL Express 和 S&S International 每天约 20 班，大约早上 7:30 到晚上 9 点，终点是峇株巴士总站。
  4. Choice B: private car transfer选择 B：包车接送Book a private car from Changi or Senai straight to your hotel in Batu Pahat. Easiest with luggage; from Singapore, allow extra time for the border crossing. Arrange it before you fly.从樟宜或士乃机场包车直接到峇株的酒店，带行李最方便；从新加坡出发要预留过关时间。请在出发前安排好。
  5. GrabCar around town市区内叫 GrabCarSame as Option 1 once you are here.到了之后和方案一一样。

Already in KL city? Express buses from TBS (Terminal Bersepadu Selatan) reach Batu Pahat Bus Terminal in 3 to 4 hours and run all day. Times here were checked in October 2026, so confirm them when you book.

人已经在吉隆坡市区？从 TBS（南部综合巴士总站）搭长途巴士到峇株巴士总站约 3 至 4 小时，全天都有班次。以上时间为 2026 年 10 月查询的资料，订票时请再确认。

Ready-made plans现成路线

Your weekend, sorted

One plan for Saturday before the wedding dinner, and one for Sunday before you head home.

星期六婚宴前一条路线，星期天回家前一条路线。

Saturday: old town food crawl星期六：老街吃货路线

Morning into lunch · all in the old town area · back in time to rest before dinner早上到中午 · 都在老街一带 · 吃完回去休息，晚上喝喜酒

  1. 亚泗云吞面 Ah See Wan Tan MeeOpens at 9am. Share a plate, there is more to come.早上 9 点开门。先分着吃，后面还有。
  2. 后巷薄饼 Fried Popiah stallA cart on the street beside Ah See, along the same row. Grab some popiah to eat with your noodles.亚泗旁边那条街、同一排店外的小档，买几条薄饼配云吞面一起吃。
  3. 顺来粿汁 Soon Lai Kway ChapNext up, a bowl of kway chap.接着来一碗粿汁。
  4. Yeo Yeo 面煎糕Phone your order in first: thin, with butter.记得先打电话预订：薄的，加牛油。
  5. 嘟嘟好炸鸡 Good Good Fried ChickenOpens at noon. Best eaten hot.中午 12 点开，趁热吃最好。
  6. Akbar CendolFinish with a cold bowl of cendol, then save room for the banquet.最后来一碗冰凉的煎蕊，然后留点肚子喝喜酒。

Sunday: one last round星期天：回家前再吃一轮

Morning to lunch · needs a car · then drive home早上到中午 · 需要开车 · 吃完就回家

  1. 汉记面包西果店 Han KeeBreakfast: the otak egg bread.早餐：必吃乌打蛋面包。
  2. Pantai Minyak BekuSea breeze and the chiselled well while it's still cool.趁凉快去吹海风、看凿石古井。
  3. 峇株吧辖广场 Dataran PenggaramA photo with the hand and chisel.和“手握凿子”合照。
  4. BP Bariani PowerBeriani for lunch. Go early, it closes at 5pm.午餐吃印度香饭，早点去，下午 5 点关门。
  5. Bite BiteCoffee and pastries for the drive home.带杯咖啡和酥皮面包上路。

    Before you go出发前

    Small-town rules

    小镇生存守则

    • Go early早点去The famous stalls open in the morning and close by mid-afternoon, or whenever they sell out.有名的档口多数早上开、下午就收，卖完也会提早关门。
    • Bring some cash, just in case带点现金，以防万一Some old stalls are cash only, and we can't promise every shop takes card or QR pay.有些老档口只收现金，我们不敢保证每家都能刷卡或扫码付款。
    • Check the rest day留意休息日Each shop rests on a different day. Arriving Thursday or Friday? Read the hours on each card first.每家店的休息日都不同。星期四、五就到的话，先看看卡片上的营业时间。
    • Drive, trust the app开车最方便A car is the easiest way around. The old town has several one-way streets, so follow Waze.在峇株自驾最方便。老街有不少单行道，跟着 Waze 走就对了。
    • Toasting? Take a GrabCar喝酒就叫 GrabCarIf you drink at the banquet, don't drive. Book a GrabCar back to your hotel.婚宴上喝了酒就别开车，叫 GrabCar 回酒店。
    • Dodge the noon sun避开正午太阳Do the outdoor stops in the morning while it's cool. Eat in the middle.户外景点安排在早上趁凉快去，中午就专心吃。
